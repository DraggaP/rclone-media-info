Rclone Media

A personal Google Drive connection maintained by DraggaP.

This app uses rclone to read and transfer the owner's files between Google Drive and a privately managed server. It is for the owner's personal use; it is not a public file-sharing service.

Google authorization lets the owner connect their own Drive. This website does not request access to Google Drive, collect files, or contain a login form.
